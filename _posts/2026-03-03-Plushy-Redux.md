For the Redux assignment, I wrote a C-program that recreates the behaviour observed in the Circuit Plushy assignment by using a button and switch to determine if the three LEDs are on or off.

A tip I would give my past self in completing this assignment would be that instead of trying to fix the formatting myself, a quick shortcut is to select all of my code, right click what is selected, and then choose the option "Format Document"! Learning and applying this trick will save me a ~ton~ of time in the future.
