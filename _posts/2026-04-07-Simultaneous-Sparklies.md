For the Simultaneous Sparklies - Mid-sem Proj 2 - assignment, I created an Arduino program that combines three firefly flashing patterns simultaneously.

A tip I would give my past self in completing this assignment would be to be better about planning when I would be able to test the Arduino program. The usb we were given in class does not connect to my personal laptop, and because I wasn't great about planning when I could make it to TCL, I found myself going late at night to check my Arduino was working, which is not great because I was close to the assignment's deadline. So, in the future, I'll be better about scheduling when I can make it to TCL.
