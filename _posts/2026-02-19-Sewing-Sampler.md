Here's a photo of the back and front of my sewing sampler I made for CSCI 103! Because this was my first time sewing, I was able to learn a lot of new, awesome stitches. For instance, using the template handed out in class, I practiced the running, back, satin, stem, cross, blanket, and whip stitches. For the project's requirement of adding two new stitches, I also learned how to do the chain and herringbone stitches using [this website](https://www.craftylity.com/10-basic-hand-embroidery-stitches-for-beginners/)!
