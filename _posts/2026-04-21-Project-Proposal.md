<ins>Description</ins>: This project simulates a high-security museum display reacting to theft. A felt copy of a Louvre jewelry necklace will glow green under normal conditions. When a black glove hovers over the piece, it casts a shadow detected by a light sensor. Only when the glove both creates this shadow and touches a conductive fabric patch (homemade button) does the system trigger an alarm. Red LEDs will blink and a buzzer will sound.

<ins>Motivation</ins>: ​As an art history major, I wanted to create a project inspired by the 2025 Louvre Jewelry Heist. Specifically, I'm really interested in how museums protect their valuable items, so in this project I explore how we can use technology to improve museum security.
