For the RGB Loop assignment, I  wrote a C-program that created a simulation of a loop where every 10th time of the loop, a different colored LED light is printed three times (Red, Green, or Blue). Through this assignment, I got practice in programming loops, and I would say in general this assignment required a lot more logic in order to determine how to write the code. 

A tip I would give my past self in completing this assignment would be to spend more time thinking through the logic of the program and planning what the code should do before actually writing the C-program. At the end of class, Professor Howley suggested we do this because this is an assignment, as said above, that requries a lot of logic, but I had ignored her advice. If I had actually listened to her helpful tip, I think this assignment would have gone a lot faster and I would have made less mistakes, so this is definitely something I will be doing in the future.
