For the Jammin' assignment, I wrote an Arduino program that plays the melody "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star," the song of my choice, when the slide switch is in the on position and stops playing when the slide switch is off. I used this [source](https://blogs.glowscotland.org.uk/sb/public/youthmusicinitiative/uploads/sites/3670/2020/09/07155606/Twinkle-Twinkle-Little-Star.pdf) to get the notes and played with different note durations to make sure I was correctly playing the melody.

A tip I would give my past self in completing this assignment would be to think of the slides as the textbook, and when confused, instead of first going to the TAs, which is what I've done in the past and still has been super helpful, I should instead first go through all the slides and see if there's any past concepts I can use to help me fix my problems. Another tip I would give myself would be to not choose overly complicated songs. At first I started with a much more complicated and longer song, but I decided to change it to this more simple song, and that made this assignment so much easier.
