For the Jammin' assignment, I wrote an Arduino program that plays "Banana Pancakes" by Jack Johnson, the song of my choice, when the slide switch is in the on position.

A tip I would give my past self in completing this assignment would be to think of the slides as the textbook, and when confused, instead of first going to the TAs, which is what I've done in the past, I should instead first go through all the slides and see if there's any past concepts I can use to help me fix my problems.
