Have you ever thought about how spaces and objects around us could respond to the people using them? In high school, I was really interested in [architecture](https://www.marlborough.org/news/~board/student-life/post/real-world-impact-a-tiny-homes-project) because I was curious how design shapes human experience, and Electronic Textiles feels like an extension of that idea but at a smaller, more personal scale. I’m excited that in Electronic Textiles, I’ll be able to explore how we can integrate technology and fabric to create pieces that are practical but will also demand creativity. Similar to the reasons I am drawn to architecture, by the end of this class, I hope to design an electronic textile  that connects, as architect Louis Sullivan put it, form (design that is meaningful) with function.
