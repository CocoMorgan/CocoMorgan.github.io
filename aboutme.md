Have you ever thought about how spaces and objects around us could respond to the people using them? My personal interests in [architecture](https://www.marlborough.org/news/~board/student-life/post/real-world-impact-a-tiny-homes-project) have made me curious about how design shapes human experience, and Electronic Textiles feels like an extension of that idea at a smaller, more personal scale. Instead of designing buildings, this class allows the material itself to become interactive. I’m excited that in Electronic Textiles, I’ll be able to explore how we can integrate technology and fabric to create pieces that are practical but also will show off my creativity. Similar to the reasons I am drawn to architecture, I hope to design an electronic textile by the end of this class that connects both meaningful and creative design with function.
